Your terminal cards, on your phone. Leave an AI coding agent such as Claude Code working on your computer, then follow it and answer it from your iPhone.

## What it does

- See every workspace and card, and which agents are waiting for you.
- Open a card, read its output and scroll back through its history.
- Type into it with a keyboard made for the terminal: quick keys for Esc, Tab, Ctrl and the arrows, saved snippets, and multi-line replies.
- Get a banner, a badge and a widget when a card needs you, even with the app closed.
- Look at the whole canvas, and move and resize cards.
- Open file cards read-only, with diffs and a Markdown preview.
- Preview a dev server running on your computer in the in-app browser.
- Find any card, workspace or action from the command palette.

## Private by design

The app connects straight to your own computer over ssh, for example through Tailscale. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking. Pairing takes one QR code: run `ift phone add` on the computer and scan it.
