# Canvas and layout

What this covers: where cards go and how they move: the placement block, tidy, dragging and snapping, undo, the selection and focus ring, the viewport and zoom, the card gap and default size, card numbers, resize, swap and slide.

Read it before touching `infiniterm-core/src/layout.rs`, `slot_snap.rs`, `resize.rs`, `swap.rs`, `slide.rs`, `cards.rs`, `model/` placement or gesture code, `viewport`, or `paint.rs`'s frame, ghost and ring drawing.

Related: `canvas-interaction.md` (mouse gestures, workspaces, switcher, masking), `settings-and-config.md` (`cards.*`, `ui.*` look settings), `terminal.md` (zoom on a card's bottom is a terminal feature but lives here: it is a viewport mode).

Moved here from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-19 with the text unchanged; CLAUDE.md keeps only the index and the rules every change must respect.

- Positions are permanent; nothing re-tiles. A new card takes the first free slot of a square block grown from the workspace's top-left corner (`layout::block_order`, `block_slot`): ring n adds column n top to bottom, stopping above the corner, then row n left to right ending on it, so 4 cards are a 2x2, 9 a 3x3, 17 is the top of column five. It does not depend on the focused card, and a hole a closed card left is filled first. The lattice is laid over the WHOLE workspace (`layout::fill_slot`, 2026-09-26), anchored at the canvas's half cell so new cards line up with old ones: first the GRID's holes (`grid_hole`), the lattice slots whose row and column already hold a card sitting on the lattice, in reading order, then growth of that grid right and down; a canvas with no card on the lattice falls back to the free slots inside the cards' box. Rows and columns of lined-up cards, not the box, because an off-lattice cluster of small cards above Ekin's grid made the box rule fill the strip beside it when the hole he meant was below #18. Counting from the canvas origin alone missed every hole in a workspace whose cards had been moved up and left of it (Personal Stuff reached x = -5237), and Cmd+T kept growing past them. Cards have the screen's shape, so the block does too and fit-all fills the window. Before 2026-09-24 a card took the free slot NEAREST the focused card, ties to the window's aspect; Ekin could not say where Cmd+T would land, which is the reason it went. No fixed column count. Nothing re-tiles on its own; `canvas.tidy` (palette only, 2026-09-24) does it when asked: the workspace's UNITS (a group, a split cluster, a loose card; `layout::tidy_units`, 2026-09-26) in reading order go back into the same block from the top-left of their bounding box, each unit moved rigidly with its inside arrangement, a column as wide as its widest unit and a row as tall as its tallest, neighbours a gutter apart with every column and row starting on a HALF-slot line of the placement grid (whole slots left 875 px beside every half card), starting from the line nearest the cards (not their frames), then fit-all. `GROUP_PAD` is half the gutter since 2026-09-27 (it was 50, twice the gutter), so a frame fits in one gutter and groups sit on the grid like cards; Cmd+Z scatters them again. Card by card it spread a group across the block and its frame ballooned over other cards. Groups move to a free block right of everything.
- Anything that reads card geometry is scoped to one workspace. Card placement treats other groups' frames as occupied. A card may not be dropped over another. Dragging a single card moves a GHOST (`Gesture::ghost`: an outline, focus-ring colour when the space is free, `warn` when not) and the card goes there on the drop (`Model::drop_card`, gliding) or stays where it never left; the card sliding under the pointer from the first pixel read as a mistake being made rather than a choice being offered. The ghost snaps to the GRID's nearest slot of its own size when one is within `SNAP_RANGE_RATIO` (10%) of its extent, else it follows the pointer on the 25 px grid (always snapping pulled a card up to 875 px and nothing sat between slots) (`slot_snap.rs`, since 2026-09-27), and the drag draws those slots faintly around it (`Model::ghost_slots`, `GHOST_SLOT_REACH`). Slots come in paper sizes, a full slot divided the way a split divides it (`pieces`: halves 850 + 25 + 850 of 1725, 1000 + 25 + 975 of 2000), so a half dragged shows half slots and every piece lines up with the full grid. It snapped to neighbours' edges before, and a canvas arranged by hand drifted off the grid one drag at a time until every card Cmd+T made landed out of line (Ekin's Streaming workspace). A drop with the ghost's centre on another card swaps the two (the arrow-swap's rule); Escape mid-drag cancels. A group drag still moves the cards live and `Model::end_gesture` puts them back on an overlap. A drag shows alignment guides on exact matches only, against the ghost while there is one. Every move, swap, drop and resize calls `Model::remember_layout` first, so `layout.undo`/`layout.redo` (Cmd+Z / Cmd+Shift+Z; an editor keeps those for its buffer) walk one trail of `UndoStep`s: `Rects` (a move) and `Closed` (a card; undo reopens it in its slot through `reopen_card`, the same path `card.reopen` uses, and takes it out of the reopen ring so Cmd+Shift+T does not bring a twin). Undo NEVER closes a card: making a card was a step (`Created`) from 2026-09-21 to 09-26, and Cmd+Z on a card Ekin had opened and started working in closed it, shell and all; there is no redo of a close either, for the same reason. `Model::undoing` keeps the reopen an undo performs off the trail. Closing a card with several tabs, browser or editor, asks first unless the card is locked (there Cmd+W is one tab); Ekin closed an editor of tabs meaning one tab. One card is a focus and wears the white ring; in a SELECTION the active card stays white so you can see where you are and the rest wear blue, all at one strength, because a command acts on all of them and a dimmed ring made it unreadable which.
- Every viewport change is animated; the system's reduced-motion switch outranks `ui.animations`. UI affordances are sized in screen pixels then divided by the zoom; the interface multiplier (`ui_scale`, Cmd+Shift+= / -) reaches every piece of chrome (card labels, the title bar and its tabs, the status bar, the palette, the dialogs, the shortcuts panel; `titlebar_h()` / `statusbar_h()` in main.rs) and never terminal text, borders or focus rings. The traffic lights stay where macOS put them.
- Rounded cards are `ui.cardRadius` (2026-10-06, #235, 0 = square): gpui's content mask is rectangular, so nothing clips a body to the rounded shape; instead every quad that reaches a card edge takes the radius itself: the outlines in `paint.rs` (`card_radius`), the body fills and dim washes through `chrome::card_body_quad` / `card_wash` (the radius reaches a body as the `CardRadius` global, set once a frame like `CardOpacity`), the label chip in the card's corner, the tab strip's top corners and the browser texture's bottom ones (`paint_image` takes radii). A new full-bleed quad at a card edge needs the same. Not rounded yet: the hover band, the editor's file tree and the diff and transcript bodies' own strips if they grow one. The space between cards is `cards.gap` (2026-10-06, #232; `Model::gap()`, default `cards::GUTTER` = 25; a group's frame pad is half of it, `Model::group_pad()`): the placement and growth functions already took the gutter as a parameter, so the setting only replaced the constant at their call sites in `model/`. Drag snapping and the grid stay 25 (`GRID_SIZE`); the lattice slots, tidy and splits follow the gap, which `the_gap_setting_spaces_splits_new_cards_and_group_frames` checks at 0, 10 and 60. A new card's size is `cards::default_size`: `cards.width` / `cards.height` in cells when set, else from the WINDOW (the default since 2026-09-24, both settings 0): the largest card of `cards.shape` ("16:9" by default, any "W:H", or "window" for the window's own shape; Ekin could not choose, so it is a setting) that fits the canvas at 100% less a margin, capped at 80 cells wide (`cards::auto_size`). 16:9 keeps a canvas the same shape on every Mac and after a window resize. The fixed 69 by 80 cells, portrait, suited only Ekin's 32-inch 4K; his mini's settings.json names 69 and 80 so his canvas did not change. The test harness pins 69 by 80 too, because the placement tests were written against it.
- Card numbers (`Card.number`, the `#7` on the label) are the LOWEST number no open card has (`Model::take_number`, `lowest_free_number`), since 2026-09-24; before, they counted up forever and a week of cards would read #2332. A closed card's number is reused, so `#7` from last week may be another card now; a card brought back by Cmd+Z or `card.reopen` whose number was taken meanwhile gets a free one rather than a twin.
- Cmd+Ctrl+Enter (`card.size.reset`) grows a card from its top-left corner into the free space beside and below it, each axis stopping a gutter short of the nearest card, up to the default size (`resize::fill_from_corner`); a quarter beside a half becomes the other half. All four corners are tried (the same rule on a mirrored layout) and the growth that fills a GAP wins: the most sides landing a gutter from a card (`resize::enclosure`), then top-left so the card stays put, then the larger. Top-left alone came first until 2026-09-26, and a split's bottom half moved under the top row grew down into open canvas instead of up into the gap it sat in (Ekin's #3); mirrors date from card #96, full height against a neighbour on its right, which said "no room" with the canvas empty to its left. With SEVERAL cards selected, `card.swap.*` (Cmd+Alt+Shift+Arrow) moves the selection as one block (`swap::move_block`, 2026-10-06, #238): the block is the selection's bounding box, the slot is that box shifted by its own size plus a gap, cards fully inside the slot go the other way into the box left behind, a card straddling the slot or a frame of a group none of the selection belongs to refuses the move. `card.slide.*` (Cmd+Ctrl+Alt+Arrow, `slide.rs`, 2026-10-08) closes the gap a resize leaves: the card moves along its row or column to one gutter from the next card in line, else to the next grid slot of its size, never over a card. Cmd+Alt+S (`card.size`, `Source::Sizes`) is the menu of fractions; a `Half` is exactly what a split makes so the two tile. Cmd+Alt+R was taken by `group.rename`.
- "Resize to window" (`card.size.window`, last row of the `card.size` menu, #585, `window_fit.rs` + `model/window_fit_cmd.rs`, 2026-10-27): every selected card keeps its width and takes the height that gives the window's aspect (`view_size`, on the grid, never under `MIN_CARD_H`), then `window_fit::reflow` places the selection in reading order of the OLD positions: x is a gap right of the nearest left neighbour's NEW right edge, y a gap below the nearest card above's NEW bottom (neighbours read from the old geometry, unselected cards included as fixed), a card with no such neighbour keeps that coordinate, so rows stay rows and columns columns; neighbours that grew push, ones that shrank pull. A closing `sweep` pushes whatever still collides right or down (shorter move) to a gap away; a grouped card keeps half a gap (the frame pad) from cards of other groups. One undo step, `soft_group_id` cleared, the view fits the selection afterwards; a maximised card refuses with a notice; other workspaces are never read. It was built as a spike with four variants (same area, same width, window-sized, scale the selection as a block); Ekin tried them on the Air and chose same width with reflow, the others were deleted. Known limits: overlap is checked per card, not per group frame, so a partial selection can leave a group's bounding frame around an unselected card; a card covered by an in-place editor (`cover.rs`) is not in the selection and stays behind; a selection with unselected cards between its own can send a card across them (kept on purpose).
- "Default size" (`card.size.default`, the row after it in the `card.size` menu, 2026-10-27): every SELECTED card takes `Model::default_size()` (`cards.width` / `cards.height`, else the window-shaped card), where "Full" acts on the active card only. It is the same reflow as "Resize to window": `window_fit::reflow_to` takes a `Target` (`Window(aspect)`: same width, the window's height; `Size(s)`: exactly `s`) and nothing else differs, including the limits above. The label lives in `window_fit_cmd::DEFAULT_ROW` (Ekin did not pick a name; a rename is that one line). Notice when nothing changes: "already the default size".
- Cmd+3 (`canvas.zoom.fitGroup`, the id kept for bindings) fits the SELECTION when several cards are selected (2026-09-29), else the focused card's group, else its block (`layout::cluster_of`: loose cards a gutter apart).
- Zoom on a card's bottom (#313, renamed from "reading mode" in #340, 2026-10-09; `reading_cmd.rs` + `reading.rs` keep the old name inside; setting `ui.cardZoom`, old `ui.readZoom` still read, 0 = off; commands `canvas.zoom.cardBottom` and `.up`/`.down`): Cmd+1 on a terminal card the view already frames (`Model::framed_card`, set by `frame_card`) zooms to `ui.readZoom` (1.5) with the card's bottom at the window's bottom, centred across; Cmd+Up/Down pan three rows and only inside the card, and mean that ONLY while `Model::reading` is set (`resolve_chord` asks first; at every other time they are the shell's). Typing snaps back to the bottom (`note_input`) and keeps the mode; Cmd+1 again, Cmd+2 or any hand pan or zoom leaves it (`Model::stop_framing` replaces every `framing = false`). Escape is not a way out: programs use it.
- The frame band under the pointer lights up (`AppView::hover_edge`, from `hit()` on every move; `paint_hover_band` in the focus ring colour at a hint's alpha) and the cursor says what a press would do: resize arrows on an edge or corner, an open hand on the move band, a closed hand during a drag. Before this a draggable frame looked like one that was not.
