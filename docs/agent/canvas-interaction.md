# Canvas interaction

What this covers: the mouse and keyboard ways of working a canvas that are not placement: the label chip as a frame target, the marquee and selection gestures, moving cards between workspaces, workspace focus and dimming, the Ctrl+Tab card switcher, protect, mask, and a terminal card in a window of its own.

Read it before touching `infiniterm-ui/src/input.rs` (mouse paths), `pan_mode.rs`, `switcher.rs` / `switcher_view.rs`, `decoys.rs`, `own_window.rs`, or the model's selection, focus-trail and workspace commands.

Related: `canvas-and-layout.md` (where cards sit and how they move), `ui-chrome.md` (menus, palette, dialogs).

Moved here from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-19 with the text unchanged.

- A drag on bare canvas draws a selection rectangle (2026-09-29, `AppView::marquee`, `Model::marquee_select` / `marquee_done`): the cards of the workspace it touches are selected live, the one nearest the press focused, set straight on the selection while dragging and landed through `focus_extended` once at the release so the trail does not fill with every mouse move; Shift+drag keeps what was selected; Escape restores the selection from before the press; a press that never passes the drag slop is still the plain deselecting click. A press on the frame or label of a card in a selection of several drags the whole selection (`GestureKind::MoveSelection`, the group drag's live move and `end_gesture`'s put-back, which spares the frames of every group a moved card is in); before, the press focused that card and dropped the selection. Cmd+click toggles a card in or out of the selection (`toggle_selected`, Finder's rule); on a lone focused card it is the body's click, so links still open. Both from Ekin the same day.
- A terminal card in a window of its own (Cmd+Shift+O, #356/#368, `own_window.rs`): the window forwards every key to the card's body and skips the keymap, except the chords whose command is in `extracted_keys::COMMANDS` (font bigger/smaller, `card.clear`, `terminal.visual`, #369): looked up in the live keymap, run with the window's card as the focused one (`AppView::run_for_extracted` puts the canvas's selection back after), so a rebinding works there and `card.clear` cannot hit the card focused on the canvas. Everything else (find, the palette, canvas, cards) is not in the window; a new command goes in that list only when it needs no UI the window lacks.
- The label chip is a FRAME target for the mouse, not a body one (`AppView::label_hits`, filled by `paint_labels` each frame): drag it to move the card, double-click it or the frame band to fit the card (Cmd+1 for the mouse), double-click bare canvas to fit everything (Cmd+2), or hold the left button on bare canvas and click the right (`pan_mode::chord_fits_all`, 2026-09-24: Ekin found the double-click also counted a left-then-right pair and asked for the held form, which has no timing window). A double-click in the body belongs to the program, except while that body is drawn as bars (`CardBody::draws_bars`: the font under the legible size, or the frame crowded; terminals only): then there is no word to select and it fits the card (`pan_mode::double_click_fits`, 2026-10-08). A click that moves the focus to a card clipped by the window's edge reveals the whole card on the RELEASE, if the press did not travel; on the press a text-selection drag would have the card slide under the pointer.
- Two fingers on the trackpad pan the canvas on every surface a card cannot use them on (2026-10-19, #451, Ekin: "two finger track should work on all surfaces", option 1 over a modifier): `AppView::wheel` routes a non-zoom scroll through the pure `pan_mode::route_wheel`. `CardBody::wheel` now returns whether the body USED the event, decided from its own room to move the way the fingers did (`pan_mode::can_scroll`, `Grid::can_scroll`: a terminal with history, or whose program owns the wheel through mouse mode or the alternate screen; an editor with a line or a column to go to; a page, diff or transcript pane not at its end; a browser page always, CEF does not say), and a body that says no leaves the gesture to the canvas (a fresh shell, a card at the end of its content, bare canvas, a frame, a label). The target is fixed at the first event of a trackpad gesture (`touch_phase` Started; momentum after the lift arrives as Moved with no new Started and keeps it), so an unused card at its end never passes the same gesture on to the canvas halfway and a pan drifting over a card never starts scrolling it; a mouse wheel (lines, no phases) decides every notch. Direction is natural: `pan_origin` moves the viewport by minus the fingers' own delta, as a Cmd+drag pan does. Cmd+scroll and the pinch stay the zoom, untouched; three-finger gestures are the system's and not handled. One `mouse.canvas.scrollpan` usage line per gesture, spaced by `ZOOM_GESTURE_GAP_MS`. Unverified: how it feels on a real trackpad. Editor sideways scroll to the right judges "a visible line is wider than the text area" counting a tab as one column.
- A card moves to another workspace from the palette only (Ekin, 2026-09-25: "a command center action", no keys): `card.moveToWorkspace` opens `Source::MoveTo`, the other workspaces with their card counts and "New workspace"; `move_selection_to` puts each selected card, in reading order, into the destination's first free block slot at its own size, out of its group, a cover with its terminal, sets the destination's `focused` to it, and leaves you where you were with the focus back on your trail. It replaced `workspace.move.next`/`.prev` (no key, followed the card, kept its rect so it could land on a card). Not on the undo trail.
- A workspace remembers its focused card beside its viewport (`Workspace::focused`), else a switch landed on whichever card was made first. `ui.unfocusedDim` washes the canvas while another app is in front, beside `ui.inactiveDim` for an unfocused card, and every card is dimmed ONCE: the wash goes under the cards and over the focused card only, because an unfocused card already wears its own scrim and one wash over the whole window dimmed those twice (2026-09-24); gpui refreshes on activation so it needs no observer. The command palette lists every card (`Card: <label>`, with the workspace after it when elsewhere) and every other workspace; Enter switches, focuses and fits. The placement menu and the phantom's kind picker keep their designed order (`Source::keeps_its_order`); recency is for lists of hundreds.
- `card.mask` (Cmd+Shift+H) draws a decoy over a card while somebody reads your screen: a second pane running `terminal.decoyCommand` at the card's size, reconciled from `Card.masked` each frame (`decoys.rs`) the way terminal bodies follow cards. Every key, click, wheel, paste and composed character goes through `AppView::live_body`, which is `None` for a masked card, so nothing reaches the decoy or the shell under it; Enter or Escape lifts it. The default is `command log stream --style compact` because zsh has a builtin `log`; a card's command runs through `zsh -lc`, login and NOT interactive, so a PATH set in `.zshrc` is absent and a Homebrew program needs its full path (`/opt/homebrew/bin/htop`), cron's rule. Under the daemon backend a decoy is an `iftd` session; one left masked at quit lives until the next launch's `kill_orphans`.
- `card.protect` (Cmd+Shift+L, `Card.protected`, saved) is not `locked` (the keyboard's): a protected card refuses Cmd+W with the way out named, its workspace will not close around it, and a shell that exits is replaced (`pane_id` cleared, `terminals.rs` spawns fresh) rather than closing the card. The chip is `warn` with a lock glyph; the tab strip does not show it yet (a note for the browser session on the roadmap).
- The card switcher (Ctrl+Tab, 2026-09-22, `switcher.rs` + `switcher_view.rs`) is Cmd+Tab for cards: hold Ctrl, Tab (or Up/Down while it is open) steps, Ctrl's release or Enter commits (the root element's `on_modifiers_changed`, no NSEvent monitor), Escape cancels, and the canvas does not move until the commit. Its rows are frozen when it opens: the card you are in, then `focus_trail` most recent first, then the rest. What makes it work is what the trail keeps: a focus you CHOSE (click, palette, `ift`, a new card, the switcher) earns its place at once, one you WALKED into with Cmd+Alt+Arrow (`Model::focus_traversing`) only when you stay `TRAIL_DWELL_MS` (1.5 s) or type into it (`note_input` from `key_down`), because Ekin crosses three cards on the way to a fourth and a plain recency list landed one press on a card he passed. The same trail is what a close walks back, so that got better too. Ctrl+Tab is a carve-out on a locked card, like Cmd+Escape.
